01 — Labour & Rework Waste (W_score)
Aggregate across all team members performing manually what could be automated or templated.
Monthly CTC ÷ 200. Use ₹300–500 for most SME roles; ₹600–900 for senior/founder time.

02 — Tool Efficiency (T_score)
Include all SaaS, subscriptions, agency tools, and cloud software costs.
If your team of 8 uses only 2 out of 6 tools actively, that's ~33%.
03 — Visibility & Decision Quality (V_score)
Inventory, pricing, hiring, client prioritisation — how many are made on gut feel vs. real data?
Decisions that stall because the right number, status, or report isn't immediately visible.
Monthly revenue ÷ (team size × 160). Rough proxy for opportunity cost of wasted time.
